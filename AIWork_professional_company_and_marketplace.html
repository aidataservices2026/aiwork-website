Our proposed workflow

Clear scope first. Quality checks throughout.

For an initial project, AIWork proposes a transparent process rather than promising unverified capacity or results.

Define the brief

Confirm language, sample volume, instructions, data permissions, acceptance criteria, deadline and budget.

Run a small pilot

Test a limited batch, review disagreements and refine instructions before committing to larger volumes.

Review and deliver

Check agreed samples, document issues and deliver in the requested format after scope and acceptance are agreed.

Transparency: AIWork is not yet presenting a completed-project portfolio, verified customer list, or production-scale team. Any future examples should be clearly labelled as demos unless they are real, permissioned client work.

About AIWork

A small initiative being built carefully.

AIWork is being developed as a concept for a service company and task marketplace. The initial focus is Telugu-English language work and human evaluation that can support AI systems.
